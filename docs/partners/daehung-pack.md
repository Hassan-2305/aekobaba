# Daehung Pack — partner data sources

Added 2026-10-10. Daehung Pack (also branded Mingda Packaging, 明达包装 / 大向包装) is a family-owned
printed flexible packaging maker in Southern China. No public website was found, so every fact on
Aekobaba cites one of the two documents they supplied, hosted with the site:

| Document                       | Hosted at                                     |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Company presentation (18 pp.)  | `/partners/daehung-pack/company-profile.pdf`  |
| Product catalog 2025 (20 pp.)  | `/partners/daehung-pack/products-2025.pdf`    |

Both files are publicly downloadable once deployed. Remove them (and point the listings'
`sourceUrl` elsewhere) if Daehung does not want them public.

## What was listed

- 10 formats from the product catalog: stand-up, box (flat-bottom), coffee, side-gusset, spout,
  vacuum, bag-in-box, refill packs, roll stock, form-fill-seal film.
  MOQ as published: 500 pcs digital / 10,000 pcs gravure; roll stock and FFS film 1 roll digital /
  5 rolls gravure. Up to 10 colours. No setup for digital; cylinders required for gravure.
- 6 formats from the presentation's product pages (no MOQ stated for these).
- No prices or lead times are published in either document → "Ask the supplier".
- Images were cropped from the documents (`/partners/daehung-pack/*.jpg`); the catalog mockups
  carry a sample "REVO" brand.
